L2 Client Escalation Portal

An internal tool for assigning escalations, tracking follow-ups, and keeping internal notes separate from customer communication.

The problem

A shared inbox carried the requests, but the email thread alone made it hard to tell who owned a ticket and when the next reply was due.

What I built

As the business analyst and builder, I built a workflow that syncs incoming tickets, routes messages using their fields, and shows SLA countdowns. The case view separates the team’s internal notes from external correspondence.

I designed the workflow so an incoming message could become an assigned ticket with a visible response deadline. Cases without a routing match stay visible for a person to assign.

Scenario
A distributor reports that a submitted order has no confirmation.
Routing
A recognizable order issue goes to order support. A message that cannot be matched stays in the triage queue for manual assignment.
Follow-up
The owner checks the response deadline, leaves the investigation in an internal note, and sends a separate customer update.

Two records, two audiences

  • Internal note“Check the status mapping with integration support. Confirmation is still pending.”
  • Customer update“We received your report and are checking the order status. We’ll update you by 3:30 p.m.”

Outcome

Tickets, assignments, notes, and reminders became visible in one place. The example shows the workflow and does not quantify any change in response time.
