01 / Integration Testing

SAP Integration Testing Tracker

Testing updates were spread across spreadsheets and follow-ups. I built a shared tracker so the teams involved could see test results, pending issues, and what still needed sign-off.

What I Owned
Workflow definition, status model, interface design, implementation, and release support.

What Changed
Status-tracking effort fell by 40% across 3 client workstreams.

02 / Escalation Operations

L2 Client Escalation Portal

Client escalations were being handled through email, making ownership and follow-ups difficult to track. I built a portal to bring tickets, assignments, internal notes, and reminders into one place.

What I Owned
Problem framing, ticket workflow, data model, interface design, and full-stack implementation.
Key Decision
Keep private working notes separate from client-facing messages while preserving a single ticket history.
What Changed
Ownership, pending actions, and reminders became visible in one operational view instead of email threads.
Next Improvement
Strengthen reporting around response time, recurring issue types, and team capacity.
